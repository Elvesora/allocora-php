# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Respect differing experience, identities, and viewpoints.
- Focus feedback on the work and its user impact.
- Protect private, customer, security, and financial information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not publish sensitive details in a public issue.
